How to connect (about 3 minutes)
  1. Tap Copy script below.
  2. Go to script.google.com (signed in to the Google account with your calendar) and tap New project.
  3. Delete everything in the code box, paste the script, and tap 💾 Save.
  4. Tap Deploy → New deployment. Click the ⚙️ next to “Select type” and choose Web app.
  5. Set Execute as: Me and Who has access: Anyone, then tap Deploy.
  6. Google asks for permission. Tap Authorize access and pick your account. If you see “Google hasn’t verified this app”, tap Advanced → Go to (your project). That’s normal, because you made the app yourself.
  7. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec), paste it below, and tap Connect.
